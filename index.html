What we do, in plain words

We build trading bots

Automated futures and same-day options strategies, for any market we can test: indices, currencies, energy, metals and rates. No gut feelings, no 3 a.m. hunches.

We try hard to break them

Most ideas look brilliant for about an hour. A team of AI agents tests, attacks and retests until the weak ones sink.

The survivors trade, carefully

Every fill is checked, loss limits act on their own, and one human signs off. The bots do not get the car keys.

The voyage

Markets move like the tide.

We enjoy the voyage.

AT THE WHEEL

Helm sets the course

The one person on board. Every size, every mix, every go is a person's call. The crew prepares; Helm decides.

TREASURE HUNT

Diver hunts for treasure

Each night a research agent tests new futures and options ideas on years of 1-minute data and real option quotes, with realistic costs, neighbouring settings and walk-forward windows. Every run is logged, failures included. Especially the failures.

CURRENT

Undertow pushes back and manages the storms

A separate red team reproduces the result and attacks it: look-ahead, unrealistic fills, overfitting, one lucky year. Most ideas stop here. The survivors get risk management: how they fit in a portfolio, daily loss limits and the worst-case drawdown for each account. Undertow is not fun at parties.

BUILD

Keel builds the boat

Survivors are coded for the futures platform or the options broker with two reviews, timing and order safety, then matched trade by trade against the research engine. If they disagree, nobody sails.

SEA TRIAL

Bosun runs the sea trial

Each strategy, futures or options, runs on its own simulation or paper account. After every close, every fill is checked against the plan and the backtest. Bosun has never missed a close.

HARBOUR

Harbor brings it to Helm

Harbor, the chief of staff, puts the evidence and the question in one brief, ranked by what matters most. Helm decides the account, the size and the loss limits. The crew advises; Helm signs.

LIGHTHOUSE

Lighthouse keeps watch

Every live strategy is checked each day against its own history and the range its backtest expects: Clear, Flagged or Blocked. A daily loss limit closes the trade and stops the account, and no account ever holds long and short at once.

THE BOAT

Trading Hub carries the crew

The add-on where every crew member's work lands: strategies set once per account, a daily loss limit that closes the trade, and never long and short at once.
